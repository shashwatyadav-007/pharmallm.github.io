Pharmacy Management System

A Pharmacy Management System is a software application designed to automate and streamline the daily operations of a pharmacy. It helps pharmacists and staff manage inventory, process prescriptions, handle billing, and maintain customer records efficiently.

Key Features:
- Inventory Management: Track stock levels, manage drug expiration dates, and automate reorder alerts.
- Prescription Management: Record and process prescriptions, verify drug interactions, and maintain prescription history.
- Billing & Invoicing: Generate bills, process payments, and support multiple payment methods.
- Customer Management: Maintain patient profiles, contact information, and purchase history.
- Reporting & Analytics: Generate sales reports, inventory reports, and track business performance.
- Supplier Management: Manage vendor information, purchase orders, and supplier contacts.
- User Access Control: Role-based access for admins, pharmacists, and staff members.

Benefits:
- Reduces manual errors and improves accuracy.
- Saves time on repetitive tasks.
- Ensures compliance with regulatory requirements.
- Provides real-time visibility into stock and sales.
- Enhances customer service through faster processing.
